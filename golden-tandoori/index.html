Our Story

A Taste of India in the Heart of Newport

Nestled on Clarence Place, Golden Tandoori has been a beloved Newport institution for decades — bringing the rich, aromatic traditions of subcontinental cooking to your table every evening. Slow-cooked curries, tandoor-fired breads and freshly blended spices, crafted with care using quality British-sourced halal ingredients.
