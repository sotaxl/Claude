Our Story

A Taste of India
in the Heart of Newport

Nestled on Clarence Place, Golden Tandoori has been a beloved fixture in Newport's dining scene for decades. We bring the rich, aromatic traditions of subcontinental cooking — slow-cooked curries, tandoor-fired breads and freshly blended spices — to your table every evening.

Every dish is crafted with care, using authentic recipes and the finest halal ingredients. Whether you're sharing a family feast or enjoying a quiet dinner for two, we promise a warm welcome and unforgettable flavours.
